    [V]a2020–2025

    On 21 Dec. 1974 the newspapers carried a notice from the Registry of Companies. It announced that a new limited company had been founded under the name Bakkafrost. Its purpose was “to operate fish production, in particular herring fillet production, commerce and other related activities.” The incorporation date was 20 Nov. 1974. The share capital, DKK 240,000. The founders of the company were stated to be, “merchant Hans Jacobsen, foreman Róland Jacobsen, both of Glyvrar, and student Martin Ingolf Jakobsen, Aarhus.”
