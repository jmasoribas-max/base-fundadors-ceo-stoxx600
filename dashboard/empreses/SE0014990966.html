    The name Lagercrantz comes from the Johan Lagercrantz trading firm, founded in 1938. From the beginning, the company focused on components and equipment, with agencies such as General Radio and Du Mont, known for their oscilloscopes at the time. Lagercrantz was acquired by Bergman & Beving in 1967.
