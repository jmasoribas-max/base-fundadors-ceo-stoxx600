    Few people were more colourful than founder and original CEO, Brian Reynolds. Brian opened the doors (of a lean-to office alongside his house in Notting Hill, England) to the inaugural incarnation of Micro Focus on 12 August 1976, to deliver what were then the first ever business-ready, off-mainframe COBOL compilers and run-time technologies to be based purely on COBOL. Later that year, co-founders Paul O’Grady (Managing Director) and Stewart Lang (Technical Director) joined him, and they began building the foundations that have made Micro Focus what it is today.
