    [V]2016–2025

    The proposed transaction is a “merger of equals”, effected by means of a public bid made on 20.01.1999 by the UK company Zeneca for all shares in the Swedish company Astra. After the concentration Zeneca will change name to AstraZeneca and the management posts will be shared equally between the parties. The new company will have its primary listing on the London Stock Exchange (with secondary listings in Stockholm and New York), and its shares will be held to 46.5% by the present Astra shareholders and to 53.5% by the present Zeneca shareholders.
